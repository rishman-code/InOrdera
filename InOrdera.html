FAQ

You probably have questions.

Will customers know they're talking to AI? ▾
InOrdera is designed to sound natural and conversational. We recommend being transparent with customers where appropriate and following applicable requirements.
What happens if InOrdera doesn't understand something? ▾
If the system encounters a situation it cannot confidently handle, your configured escalation process can transfer the interaction to your team.
Will I have to change my phone number? ▾
The answer depends on your current phone setup. InOrdera can be configured around supported phone arrangements so that you can keep disruption to a minimum.
Does it work with my POS? ▾
InOrdera supports selected integrations. View the integrations section or contact us to confirm compatibility with your system.
Do I need technical knowledge? ▾
No. Our team handles the initial configuration and helps you get set up.
Can it take complicated orders? ▾
InOrdera is designed to understand natural conversations, modifications and common restaurant ordering scenarios. When a request falls outside its configured capabilities, it can escalate to your team.
Can I change what it says? ▾
Yes. Restaurant-specific menus, rules, tone and ordering behaviour can be configured.
How much does it cost? ▾
It depends on your call volume, menu size and number of locations, so we work out pricing with you rather than publishing a fixed rate. Talk to us and we'll put together numbers for your restaurant.

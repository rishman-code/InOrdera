Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat commission in the UK (2026): what restaurants really pay

By the InOrdera team · Last checked 26 November 2026 · 6 minute read

The short answer When the app's own couriers deliver, UK restaurants typically pay around 30% of each order plus VAT. Uber Eats publishes 30% (13% if you deliver yourself), Just Eat charges around 14% plus VAT when you deliver yourself, and Deliveroo agrees rates privately, with restaurants reporting around 30% to 33%. On a £20 order that can mean £6 or more going to the app before you pay for food, staff or packaging.

Many restaurants raise their app menu prices to cover commission. Which? found one restaurant's order cost £38.04 on Deliveroo against £26.42 ordered direct. That keeps your margin, but it pushes price-sensitive customers away.

How to keep more of each order

  1. Use the apps to find customers and your own channels to keep them. Put your phone number and website on every bag, menu and receipt so a second order comes to you direct.
  2. Answer every phone call. A missed call on a busy Friday often becomes an app order instead. Phone orders pay no commission.
  3. Take orders on your own website. Customers who prefer to tap rather than talk still order direct.
  4. Use cheaper app options where they exist. Uber Eats charges 13% for pick-up and self-delivery instead of 30%.
  5. Price your app menu with care. A small markup can cover commission, but large gaps send customers elsewhere.
  6. Check your invoices every month. Promotions, marketing fees and adjustments add up on top of headline commission.

Frequently asked questions

How much commission does Deliveroo take from restaurants in the UK?

Deliveroo does not publish its UK commission rates; they are agreed with each restaurant. Restaurants have reported paying around 30% when Deliveroo riders deliver, with one telling Which? it paid 30% on orders over £25 and 33% below that.

What is the Uber Eats commission rate in the UK?

Uber Eats' published UK fees are 30% of the order when Uber couriers deliver (33% for Uber One members' orders), 13% if you deliver yourself and 13% for pick-up orders, all excluding VAT. There is also a £650 (excl. VAT) activation fee, which can be reduced by opting out of some services.

How much does Just Eat charge restaurants?

Just Eat charges around 14% commission plus VAT on each order when you deliver yourself, plus a small per-order fee. Rates are higher when Just Eat's own couriers deliver.

Is VAT charged on delivery app commission?

Yes. The published UK rates are quoted excluding VAT, so 20% VAT is added to the commission. VAT-registered restaurants can usually reclaim it; restaurants that are not VAT-registered cannot, which makes the real cost higher.

How can a takeaway pay less commission?

Move repeat customers to ordering direct by phone or on your own website, where no platform commission is charged. Use the apps for finding new customers, use cheaper self-delivery or pick-up options where they exist, and make sure every phone call is answered so direct orders are not lost.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does setup actually take? ▾
Most restaurants are live within 60 minutes. You share your menu and phone number, we import everything, you test a few calls, and you're live before the dinner rush. Our team does it with you — no IT project, no engineer needed.
Does it actually sound human? ▾
Yes. YumDial is built on the latest conversational AI voice technology, trained specifically on UK accents, regional dialects, and food service vocabulary. Most callers don't know they're speaking to AI — unless you tell them.
What happens if a caller asks something it can't handle? ▾
YumDial detects when a caller needs a human and transfers the call cleanly to your team with a brief handoff note. You're always in control. Edge cases don't get dropped.
Does it work with my existing POS system? ▾
YumDial integrates with all major UK POS systems including Lightspeed, Square, Epos Now, and others. Orders flow directly into your kitchen display or till — no double-entry, no missed tickets.
How does allergen flagging work? ▾
YumDial automatically identifies all 14 UK allergens in your menu data. When a customer mentions an allergy or requests a modification, the AI flags it, notes it on the order, and creates a timestamped audit record — giving you the documented trail that Owen's Law will require.
Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime? ▾
Monthly rolling subscription. No long-term contract. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We're confident you won't want to — but there's no lock-in.

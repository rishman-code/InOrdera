Cookie Policy

How InOrdera uses cookies and similar technologies

Last updated: 23 April 2026  ·  InOrdera Ltd

In short: We use strictly necessary cookies to operate the site, and optional analytics cookies to improve it. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow websites to recognise your device on subsequent visits, remember your preferences, and understand how you use the site.

Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and web beacons. This policy covers all of these.

2. Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies — these are required for InOrdera to function. You cannot disable them.

Name Purpose Duration
io_session Maintains your login session in the InOrdera dashboard Session
io_csrf Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks Session
io_cookie_consent Stores your cookie consent preferences 12 months

Analytics Cookies — these help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. These are only set with your consent.

Name Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique users. Data is anonymised and aggregated. 2 years
_ga_* Google Analytics Stores session state 2 years

Marketing Cookies — we currently do not use any marketing or targeting cookies on inordera.com.

3. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner: When you first visit inordera.com, you can accept or decline optional cookies via our consent banner.
  • Browser settings: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent InOrdera from working correctly.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

To change your cookie preferences after your initial visit, clear your browser cookies and revisit the site — the consent banner will reappear.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some of our pages may embed third-party content (such as YouTube videos for demos or LinkedIn social share buttons). These third parties may set their own cookies when you interact with their content. We do not control these cookies and encourage you to review the relevant third-party privacy policies.

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. We currently do not respond to DNT signals, as there is no agreed industry standard. Our cookie consent mechanism gives you equivalent control.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Where we materially change how we use cookies, we will re-request your consent.

7. Contact

For any questions about our use of cookies: privacy@inordera.com
